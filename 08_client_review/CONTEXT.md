## Job
Provide the appropriate client or stakeholder review after the independent
check. For a public demonstration with no client, this stage may instead be
used as an optional dry run or deliberately skipped with the reason recorded.

## Steps
1. For an engagement, follow the agreed review/sign-off process and record the response in `output/`.
2. For a demonstration, if the actuary wants a dry run, share the checked report with an appropriate reader and record the reaction.
3. If no review is applicable or desired, record the deliberate skip and rationale.

## Done when
The applicable review is recorded, or a deliberate skip and its rationale are
logged, ready for `99_close/`.
